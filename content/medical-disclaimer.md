# Medical disclaimer.
Please read this before relying on anything on this website for decisions about your health.

## General information only
Everything on this website, including treatment pages, symptom guides, checklists and FAQs, is general information for education. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment plan or a substitute for a consultation with a qualified doctor who has examined you.

## Every person is different
Whether a treatment is suitable, and what recovery looks like, depends on your own condition, tests and overall health. The descriptions on this website are typical and will not apply to everyone.

## Interactive tools
The "Is this for me?" checklists and symptom tools only help you decide whether to talk to a doctor. They do not assess risk and their results are not medical advice. Your answers are not stored or sent unless you choose to send them.

## No guarantee of results
No surgery or procedure has a guaranteed result. Benefits and risks are explained to you in person before any treatment, and the decision is always yours.

## Emergencies
If you have sudden chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting or signs of a stroke, do not use this website or wait for an appointment. Call 108 or 112 and go to the nearest emergency department straight away.
