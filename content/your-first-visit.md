# Your first visit, step by step.
Knowing what happens takes away half the worry. Here is what to bring, what I will do, and how you leave with a clear plan.

## Every heart has a story.

### I listen first.
What do you feel? Since when? What worries you most? Your words become my notes. Family members are welcome to sit in.

### I look closely.
Pulse, blood pressure, heart sounds and an ECG. Then only the tests you actually need, nothing more.

### You plan together.
Every option laid out in plain words, with what each one means for you. You decide, at your own pace.

## Small things that make it easier.
Practical details for the day of your visit.

### Explained in your language
I explain your condition and options in Hindi or English, without jargon. Ask as many questions as you need.

### Come a little early
Arrive 10 to 15 minutes before your time to register and settle in.

### Fees and payment
Please ask the clinic about consultation fees and payment options when you book.

### Medicines on the day
Take your usual medicines unless you have been told otherwise. Do not stop blood thinners on your own. If a test needs fasting, the clinic will tell you when you book.

## FAQs

**Do I need a referral to see Dr. Yadav?**
No. You can book directly by phone or WhatsApp. If another doctor has written a letter or notes, please bring them.

**What should I bring to my first visit?**
All earlier reports: ECG, echo, TMT, blood tests, angiography CD or report, discharge summaries, and a list of the medicines you take now.

**Does every heart problem need surgery?**
No. Many heart and vessel problems are managed with medicines and lifestyle changes. Surgery or a procedure is advised only when it is the right option for you, and the reasons are explained first.

**Can a family member come with me?**
Yes. Many decisions about the heart are family decisions, and we are happy to explain everything to them too.

**Is insurance or Ayushman Bharat accepted?**
Please call the clinic to check your plan before your visit.

**What if it is an emergency?**
For sudden chest pain, severe breathlessness, fainting or signs of a stroke, call 108 or 112 straight away and go to the nearest emergency department. Do not wait for a clinic appointment.
