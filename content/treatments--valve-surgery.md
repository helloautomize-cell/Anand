# Valve repair and replacement.
When a heart valve leaks or becomes narrow, the heart has to work harder. Valve surgery repairs your own valve where possible, or replaces it with a new one.

## Tick what sounds like you.
A quick way to see if this is worth discussing with Dr. Yadav. It is not a diagnosis, and nothing is sent unless you choose to.

## Before, during and after.

### Getting ready
- Tests: echo, ECG, chest X-ray, blood tests, and sometimes an angiography.
- Dental check: any tooth infection is treated before valve surgery.
- The choice: repair or replacement, and which type of valve, discussed with you and your family.

### The operation
- You are fully asleep under general anaesthesia.
- The heart-lung machine takes over while the valve is worked on.
- Your valve is repaired, or replaced with a mechanical or tissue valve.
- The heart is restarted and checked carefully before closing.

### Recovery
- A short stay in the ICU, then the ward.
- Early walking and breathing exercises with a physiotherapist.
- If you have a mechanical valve, regular blood tests to adjust blood thinners.
- Follow-up echo scans to check the valve.

## FAQs

**Repair or replacement, which is better?**
Where a valve can be repaired well, repair is often preferred, but it is not always possible. The choice depends on the valve and how it is damaged, and it is explained before surgery.

**Mechanical or tissue valve?**
Mechanical valves are durable but need lifelong blood thinners. Tissue valves usually do not need them long term but may wear out over time. Age, lifestyle and plans for pregnancy all matter.

**What is rheumatic heart disease?**
It is valve damage left behind by rheumatic fever in childhood. It is still common in India and often affects the mitral valve.

**How long is the hospital stay?**
It varies with your recovery and other conditions. You will get an estimate at the consultation.
