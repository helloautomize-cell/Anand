## From first visit to back home.

### An unhurried conversation
Dr. Yadav goes through your story, your old reports and your medicines, and examines you.

### Only the tests you need
Usually an ECG and an echo to start. More tests only if they will change the plan. All tests are done at the clinic.

### A plan you can take home
Your options are explained in plain words, including medicines, a procedure or surgery, and what each one means for you.

### The day of your procedure
Surgery takes place at the hospital. The team explains each step to you and your family before you go in.

### Recovery, step by step
Walking, breathing exercises and medicines, with clear guidance on what is normal and what needs a call.

### Home, with follow-up
Review visits at the clinic and reports on WhatsApp, so you are never left wondering what comes next.

## FAQs

**Will I definitely need surgery?**
Not always. Many heart and vessel conditions are first treated with medicines, lifestyle changes or a procedure through a tube. Surgery is advised only when it is the right option for you, and the reasons are explained clearly.

**Can I get a second opinion on surgery advised elsewhere?**
Yes. Bring your reports, angiography CD and prescriptions. Dr. Yadav will go through them with you and explain what he would advise and why.

**Where does the surgery take place?**
Consultations are at Anand Heart Center. Procedures and surgery take place at the hospital where Dr. Yadav operates, which will be discussed with you. [CONFIRM: hospital wording]

**How long will I stay in hospital?**
It depends on the procedure and how you recover. You will be told what to expect for your own treatment before you decide.

**Is my insurance accepted?**
Many insurance and government schemes are accepted at the hospital. Please share your policy details with the clinic and we will check for you. [CONFIRM: schemes]
