# Lung and chest surgery.
Operations on the lungs, the lining around them, the chest wall and the space between the lungs. Here is what to expect, from the first scan to recovery.

## Tick what sounds like you.
A quick way to see if this is worth discussing with Dr. Yadav. It is not a diagnosis, and nothing is sent unless you choose to.

## Before, during and after.

### Getting ready
- Tests: CT scan, lung function tests and blood tests.
- Breathing: exercises and stopping smoking help recovery.
- The plan: what will be done and why, explained before you agree.

### The operation
- You are fully asleep under general anaesthesia.
- The surgery is done through the side of the chest, sometimes with a camera.
- A chest drain is usually placed to help the lung re-expand.

### Recovery
- Breathing exercises begin early, with a physiotherapist.
- The chest drain is removed once it is no longer needed.
- Pain is managed so you can breathe deeply and walk.
- Follow-up visits and scans as needed.

## Good to know in advance.
What to bring, who looks after you, and the questions worth asking.

### Who looks after you
- Dr. Anand Yadav
- Anaesthetist
- ICU nurses
- Physiotherapist
- Pathologist

### What to bring
- CT scan films or CD
- Chest X-rays
- Lung function results
- Your current medicines list

### Questions to ask
- What is the shadow on my scan?
- Is surgery needed now?
- How much lung is removed?
- How long will the drain stay?

### After surgery
- Breathing exercises every day
- Stop smoking completely
- Walk a little more each day
- Keep follow-up visits

## FAQs

**Will I be able to breathe normally after lung surgery?**
Most people adapt well, but it depends on how much lung is involved and your lung function before surgery. This is tested and explained first.

**What is a chest drain?**
A soft tube that lets air or fluid out of the chest so the lung can expand. It is removed once it is no longer needed.

**Is every lung shadow cancer?**
No. Shadows have many causes, including infection. Tests help find the cause before any decision.

**How long is the hospital stay?**
It depends on the operation and your recovery. You will get an estimate at the consultation.
