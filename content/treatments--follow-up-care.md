# Follow-up care.
Surgery is one day. Recovery is weeks and months. Regular follow-up helps you heal well, adjust medicines, and get back to the life you want.

## Tick what sounds like you.
A quick way to see if this is worth discussing with Dr. Yadav. It is not a diagnosis, and nothing is sent unless you choose to.

## Before, during and after.

### At home
- Wound: keep it clean and dry, and watch for redness or discharge.
- Medicines: take them exactly as written in your discharge summary.
- Walking: short, regular walks that build up each day.

### At the clinic
- Your wound, heart rhythm and breathing are checked.
- Medicines and doses are reviewed and adjusted.
- Questions about diet, work, travel and driving are answered.

### Staying well
- Cardiac rehab to rebuild strength safely.
- Regular reviews and tests as advised.
- Control of sugar, BP and cholesterol.
- A plan for what to do if symptoms return.

## Good to know in advance.
What to bring, who looks after you, and the questions worth asking.

### Who looks after you
- Dr. Anand Yadav
- Clinic nurses
- Physiotherapist
- Dietitian
- Your family

### What to bring
- Your discharge summary
- All current medicines
- A note of your BP and sugar
- Your questions

### Call the clinic if
- The wound is red, hot or leaking
- You have fever
- Breathlessness is getting worse
- Swelling in the legs increases

### Daily habits
- Walk every day
- Take medicines on time
- Eat less salt and oil
- No smoking

## FAQs

**When is my first follow-up?**
Usually soon after discharge. The exact date will be written on your discharge summary.

**When can I drive again?**
It depends on the surgery and your recovery. I will tell you at your follow-up.

**When can I go back to work?**
It depends on the kind of work you do and how you are healing. You will get guidance for your situation.

**What if I feel unwell at night?**
For chest pain, severe breathlessness or fainting, call 108 or 112 straight away and go to the nearest emergency department.

**Is insurance accepted?**
Many insurance plans cover these treatments, subject to the terms of your policy. Please call the clinic to check your plan.
