# Minimally invasive surgery.
For suitable patients, some heart operations can be done through a smaller cut between the ribs instead of opening the breastbone. Here is when it is an option and when it is not.

## Tick what sounds like you.
A quick way to see if this is worth discussing with Dr. Yadav. It is not a diagnosis, and nothing is sent unless you choose to.

## Before, during and after.

### Is it right for you?
- Tests: echo, CT scan and other tests to check your anatomy.
- Suitability: not every heart or condition is suitable, and you will be told plainly.
- The plan: what is gained and what the risks are, compared with standard surgery.

### The operation
- You are fully asleep under general anaesthesia.
- The surgeon works through a small cut between the ribs.
- Special instruments and a camera may be used.
- If needed for safety, the operation can be changed to a standard approach.

### Recovery
- A short ICU stay, then the ward.
- Walking and breathing exercises start early.
- Recovery time varies, and you will be guided at each visit.
- Regular follow-up as for any heart operation.

## FAQs

**Is keyhole surgery suitable for everyone?**
No. It depends on the condition, your anatomy and your overall health. Standard surgery is still the right choice for many people.

**Is it safer than standard surgery?**
Not automatically. Both have benefits and risks. The aim is to choose the approach that is right for you.

**Will the scar be smaller?**
Usually the cut is smaller and placed at the side of the chest. Where exactly depends on the operation.

**Is recovery faster?**
Some people return to activity sooner, but this varies. You will get guidance based on your own recovery.
