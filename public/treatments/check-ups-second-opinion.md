# Check-ups and second opinions.
Not every visit leads to surgery. Many people come to understand their reports, check their symptoms, or hear a second view before a big decision. Every visit starts with listening.

## Tick what sounds like you.
A quick way to see if this is worth discussing with Dr. Yadav. It is not a diagnosis, and nothing is sent unless you choose to.

## Before, during and after.

### Getting ready
- Gather reports: ECG, echo, TMT, blood tests, angiography and discharge summaries.
- Medicines: bring the strips or a list of what you take.
- Questions: write them down, and bring a family member if you like.

### Listening first
- Dr. Yadav listens to your story and examines you.
- Your reports are reviewed together, in plain words.
- Any further tests are explained, with the reason for each.

### A clear plan
- You leave knowing your options, including no surgery where that is right.
- A written plan for medicines, tests or the next visit.
- WhatsApp the clinic if questions come up later.

## Good to know in advance.
What to bring, who looks after you, and the questions worth asking.

### Who looks after you
- Dr. Anand Yadav
- Clinic staff
- Your family

### What to bring
- All earlier reports
- Angiography CD if done
- Medicine strips or a list
- Any discharge summaries

### Questions to ask
- What do my reports show?
- Do I need treatment now?
- What are all my options?
- What happens if I wait?

### After the visit
- Follow the written plan
- Book tests if advised
- Keep a folder of reports
- Message us with questions

### Can't visit yet?
Send your reports on WhatsApp and the clinic will tell you how a review can be arranged.

## FAQs

**Is a second opinion rude to my current doctor?**
No. A second opinion is a normal and sensible step before a big decision.

**Will I be pushed into surgery?**
No. Surgery is advised only when it is the right option, and you can take time to decide.

**Can my reports be reviewed on WhatsApp?**
You can share them on WhatsApp, and the clinic will tell you how the review works. [CONFIRM]

**Do I need a referral?**
No. You can book directly by phone or WhatsApp.
